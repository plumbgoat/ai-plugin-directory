# Contributing to Plugboard

Plugboard is a **curated** directory of AI coding add-ons, organized by the
job you're doing. Suggestions are very welcome.

## Suggest an add-on

Open an issue using the **"Suggest an add-on"** template (or click the
**Suggest an add-on** button on the site). Include:

- the add-on's name and a one-line description of what it does,
- whether it's a **plugin** (tool-specific) or an **MCP server** (universal),
- a link, and
- which job section it best fits.

## What gets added

Because the list is curated (not exhaustive), not every suggestion is added —
the goal is to help people *pick*, not to list everything. Add-ons that are
genuinely useful, reasonably maintained, and fit a clear job are the best fit.

Thanks for helping keep the list good.
